Billing

Refunds, plainly.

Foodnance bills monthly. Here's exactly what happens to your money if you cancel, downgrade, or we get a charge wrong.

The short version

  • Cancel any time. You keep access through the end of the month you've already paid for.
  • We don't refund that month, whether you stop using it or just change your mind.
  • If we charge you wrong, we fix it — no need to convince us.

No refunds for the month you're in

Foodnance is billed monthly, in advance, in Canadian dollars, with applicable tax added at checkout. When you cancel, billing stops going forward — it doesn't undo the payment you already made. You keep full access until the end of the period you paid for, then your account closes.

We don't offer refunds for not using the Service, changing your mind, or switching to something else. This is the same position taken by the other invoice and inventory software in this space.

Billing mistakes are different

If we charge you the wrong amount, charge you twice for the same month, or charge you after your cancellation should already have taken effect — that's our mistake, not a request you have to justify. Email billing@foodnance.com and we'll correct it: the erroneous amount refunded to your original payment method within 7 business days.

Downgrading your plan

Moving to a lower plan takes effect at your next billing date. We don't refund the difference for the period already billed at the higher rate.

If we're the ones who end it

If we end your subscription for our own reasons — not because you broke the rules — or change our terms in a way you don't accept, you get a full refund of your most recent payment, not a prorated one.

Questions

Write to billing@foodnance.com for anything about a charge, or hello@foodnance.com for everything else.
