## Full description

Foodnance is food costing software built for independent restaurants, bakeries, cafes and commissaries. It was made by a restaurant manager with over ten years of kitchen experience.

The idea is simple: your invoices already contain your real costs, so Foodnance reads them for you.

**How it works**
1. Photograph a supplier invoice on your phone, or upload the PDF.
2. Foodnance reads every line: product, pack size and unit price. It flags a total that does not add up, so a typo never becomes a wrong food cost.
3. You check the lines and approve. Nothing counts toward your costs until you do.
4. Recipe costs, dish margins and your monthly profit and loss update automatically.

**What you get**
- **Invoice reading:** photo or PDF in, itemised lines out, with a check before anything is saved.
- **Price Movers:** see exactly which items went up, by how much, and which supplier is cheapest for the same item.
- **Recipe and dish costing:** cost every recipe from live ingredient prices, then build finished products from recipes. When flour goes up, every affected margin updates itself.
- **Monthly P&L:** profit and loss from real purchases.
- **Pro plan extras:** inventory, stock counts by storage area (works on a phone, even offline), the cost of any variance, true cost of goods based on stock counts, and staff food-safety certification tracking.

**Pricing**
Two plans, priced in Canadian dollars per location per month, with no setup fee and no annual contract. Essentials is $79 a month. Pro is $150 a month. Both start with a 14-day free trial.

**Good to know**
The AI reads each invoice line and can make mistakes, so every invoice waits for someone at your kitchen to confirm it. We are onboarding our first businesses hands-on, so you get help setting up.
